## Enhanced Autonomous Report — Final Plan

### Two UX Fixes

**Fix 1: No "Submitting Orchestration" screen.** When user clicks "Run Pipeline", immediately transition to the running state (neon DAG). Fire `SUBMIT_ORCHESTRATION` in the background — once it returns the run_id, start polling. The user never sees a spinner/loading screen between clicking the button and seeing the agent network.

**Fix 2: Report renders AFTER pipeline completes.** All visualization SQL queries fire AFTER the orchestration pipeline finishes (status = COMPLETED), not in parallel. The report is one cohesive document — visualizations interleaved with the AI narrative from exec_report, organized by the storyboard's DETECT → EXPLAIN → PREDICT → ACT → COMMUNICATE flow.

### Report Flow (per persona)

The report follows the storyboard's 5-step structure. Each step has a section header, relevant visualizations, and the AI narrative from the exec_report woven in. **Visualizations are persona-scoped** — filtered by `PERSONA_OWNER` or department.

**Step 1 — DETECT** "What just changed across my portfolio?"
1. **Portfolio KPI Banner** — anomalies count, revenue at stake, departments affected, stockout rate
   - Source: `FACT_DEMAND_RISK` WHERE `IS_CURRENT_WEEK = TRUE` filtered by persona
2. **Ranked Anomaly Table** — top anomalies ranked by revenue impact
   - Source: `FACT_DEMAND_RISK` WHERE `IS_CURRENT_WEEK = TRUE` filtered by persona
   - Styled table: rank, sub-category, region, deviation %, impact $, severity badge, direction
3. **Portfolio Deviation Heatmap** — regions × sub-categories colored by demand deviation
   - Source: `DT_DEMAND_WEEKLY` for current fiscal week, filtered by department
   - Plotly heatmap with red/amber/white/green zones
4. **Variance Distribution Histogram** — statistical shape of portfolio health
   - Source: `DT_DEMAND_WEEKLY` deviation distribution
   - Plotly bar chart with normal/watch/anomaly bands + stats sidebar
5. **AI Narrative** — the exec_report headline + key findings for this persona's section

**Step 2 — EXPLAIN** "Why did this happen?"
1. **Signal Attribution Chart** — driver decomposition (Weather, Promotion, Competitor, Digital)
   - Source: `FACT_DRIVER_ATTRIBUTION` filtered by department
   - Plotly horizontal stacked bar per category, with confidence scores
2. **AI Narrative** — exec_report drivers and root cause analysis

**Step 3 — PREDICT** "How long will this last?"
1. **Recovery Economics Timeline** — recoverable value decay over time for top risks
   - Source: `FACT_RISK_RECOVERY_CURVE` for top 3-5 risk events
   - Plotly line chart: value at risk vs days, with intervention cost overlay
2. **AI Narrative** — exec_report trajectory and predictions

**Step 4 — ACT** "What should we do?"
1. **Recommended Actions Cards** — from exec_report, with approval badges, impact/cost, confidence bars
   - Already built in current page, will enhance styling
2. **AI Narrative** — exec_report recommended_actions and risks

**Step 5 — COMMUNICATE** "Enterprise summary"
1. **Enterprise Summary Banner** — consolidated KPIs from exec_report.enterprise_summary
2. **Cross-Department Contentions** — if any
3. **Pending Approvals** — if any
4. **Validation Summary** — verdict + caveats (always shown)
