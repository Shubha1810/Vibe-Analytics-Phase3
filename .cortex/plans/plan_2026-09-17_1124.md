## Enhanced Autonomous Report — Implementation Plan

### Architecture
- **Parallel execution**: When user clicks "Run Pipeline", fire two things simultaneously:
  1. `SUBMIT_ORCHESTRATION` → start the agent pipeline (5-10 min)
  2. 6-8 direct SQL queries → fetch visualization data (~5 seconds)
- Charts render immediately. AI narrative sections overlay once the pipeline completes.
- All visualization SQL queries are READ-only against fact tables — no interference with the orchestration pipeline.

### Visualization Sections (from storyboard, mapped to SQL)

**1. Portfolio Deviation Heatmap** (Step 1 - Detect)
- Source: `DT_DEMAND_WEEKLY` filtered by current fiscal week + persona's department
- Chart: Plotly heatmap — regions (rows) × sub-categories (columns), colored by `WTD_DEMAND_DEVIATION_PCT`
- Color scale: green (over-forecast ≥20%), amber (watch ±10-19%), white (normal ±10%), red (under-forecast)

**2. Ranked Anomaly List** (Step 1 - Detect)
- Source: `FACT_DEMAND_RISK` WHERE `IS_CURRENT_WEEK = TRUE` AND `PERSONA_OWNER` matches
- Chart: Styled table with rank, sub-category, region, deviation %, impact USD, days, direction, confidence
- Top 3 highlighted as high-impact with tinted rows

**3. Variance Distribution Histogram** (Step 1 - Detect)
- Source: `DT_DEMAND_WEEKLY` — distribution of `WTD_DEMAND_DEVIATION_PCT` across all SKUs
- Chart: Plotly bar chart with color zones (green normal band, amber watch, red anomaly)
- Side panel: portfolio health stats (mean, median, std dev, skew)

**4. Cross-Department Signal Strip** (Step 1 - Detect)
- Source: `FACT_DEMAND_RISK` grouped by `PERSONA_OWNER`, top anomaly per persona
- Chart: 3-5 persona cards showing portfolio scope, anomaly counts, top signal
- Common signal forces row at bottom (Weather, Promotion, Competitor, Digital)

**5. Signal Attribution (Driver Decomposition)** (Step 2 - Explain)  
- Source: `FACT_DRIVER_ATTRIBUTION` filtered by department + current period
- Chart: Plotly horizontal bar — stacked driver contributions (Weather, Promotion, Competitor, Digital, Residual)
- With confidence badges and multicollinearity flags

**6. Recovery Economics Timeline** (Step 3 - Predict)
- Source: `FACT_RISK_RECOVERY_CURVE` for top risk events
- Chart: Plotly line chart — recoverable value vs. days from now, with intervention cost overlay
- Action window highlighted

**7. Enterprise Summary KPI Banner** (Step 5 - Communicate)
- Source: `FACT_DEMAND_RISK` aggregate + exec_report enterprise_summary
- Chart: KPI cards (already exists) but enhanced with real data: total anomalies, revenue at stake, departments affected

**8. AI Narrative Sections** (from exec_report)
- Rendered once the orchestration pipeline completes
- Per-persona sections with headline, caveats, recommended actions (existing code)
